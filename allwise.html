AllWISE#

Catalog: AllWISE, queried through VizieR as table II/328/allwise (see CDS / VizieR).

Wavelengths: W1 (3.4 um), W2 (4.6 um), W3 (12 um), W4 (22 um).

AllWISE provides detections based on WISE data alone, and is helpful for providing upper limits by finding the brightest neighbour.

Matching and aggregation#

%_ALLWISE_all.fits: %_coords.csv
	# match to AllWISE
	# convert Vega magnitudes to mJy fluxes
	# AllWISE is providing detections based on WISE data alone
	#  and is helpful for providing upper limits, by finding the brightest
	stilts cdsskymatch cdstable=II/328/allwise  \
		find=all in=$< ra=RA dec=DEC \
		radius=20 out=$@ \
		ocmd='addcol WISE1 "W1mag>0 ? (pow(10, -(W1mag+2.699+48.60)/(2.5)))*1e26: -99."' \
		ocmd='addcol WISE1_err "e_W1mag>0 ? (pow(10, -(W1mag-e_W1mag+2.699+48.60)/(2.5)))*1e26-WISE1: -99."' \
		ocmd='addcol WISE2 "W2mag>0 ? (pow(10, -(W2mag+3.339+48.60)/(2.5)))*1e26: -99."' \
		ocmd='addcol WISE2_err "e_W2mag>0 ? (pow(10, -(W2mag-e_W2mag+3.339+48.60)/(2.5)))*1e26-WISE2: -99."' \
		ocmd='addcol WISE3 "W3mag>0 ? (pow(10, -(W3mag+5.174+48.60)/(2.5)))*1e26: -99."' \
		ocmd='addcol WISE3_err "e_W3mag>0 ? (pow(10, -(W3mag-e_W3mag+5.174+48.60)/(2.5)))*1e26-WISE3: -99."' \
		ocmd='addcol WISE4 "W4mag>0 ? (pow(10, -(W4mag+6.620+48.60)/(2.5)))*1e26: -99."' \
		ocmd='addcol WISE4_err "e_W4mag>0 ? (pow(10, -(W4mag-e_W4mag+6.620+48.60)/(2.5)))*1e26-WISE4: -99."' \

For the upper-limit handling, all AllWISE sources within 20 arcsec are matched (find=all); groupsumWISE.py then sums the fluxes of all matches per input source (flux sum, error as norm):

%_ALLWISE_sum.fits: groupsumWISE.py %_coords.csv %_ALLWISE_all.fits
	python3 $^ $@

  • The Vega magnitudes are converted to fluxes (AB offsets 2.699, 3.339, 5.174, 6.620 for W1-W4, and 48.60 to convert to flux units).

  • If none of the matches has a detection in a band, the 5 sigma all-sky sensitivity is used as an upper limit (68, 111, 860, 5700 mJy for W1-W4, from the AllWISE exposure support).

Cuts applied#

In the final merge (the final rule), the WISE fluxes of the Legacy Survey deblending (LU_flux_w*_LS) are preferred, and the AllWISE measurements are only used as fallback:

  • Isolated, good extraction (isolatedLS && goodfitsW1/W2): the LS-deblended flux is used (origin LS10).

  • Blended (fracflux_w* > 1) or AllWISE brighter than the LS flux plus its error budget: the summed AllWISE flux of all neighbours is used (origin AllWISE; negative error convention).

  • Otherwise: the LS flux inflated by its error and the fracflux fraction is used as a conservative 3 sigma upper limit (origin LS10UL).

  • W3/W4 are only used when W1/W2 are unblended and isolated (!W34_blended), because the LS W3/W4 deblending is unreliable.

The origin of each WISE1/WISE2 value is recorded in the WISE1_origin and WISE2_origin columns (LS10, AllWISE or LS10UL); see Output files for the column convention.

References#

  • Cutri et al. 2013 (AllWISE Data Release): the catalog and the explanatory supplement, which documents the catalog columns and the all-sky sensitivities.

  • Wright et al. 2010 (AJ 140, 1868): the WISE mission and the Vega zero points underlying the W1-W4 magnitude system.
